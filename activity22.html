Daily Fitness Routine

Welcome to your ultimate fitness dashboard. Consistency is key! Follow the routine cards below to keep your physical health and mental clarity on track every single day.

Cardio Warm-up

Duration: 10 mins

Start with light jogging, jumping jacks, or dynamic stretching to prepare your muscles.

Strength Training

Duration: 30 mins

Focus on compound movements like squats, push-ups, and lunges to build full-body power.

Core & Flexibility

Duration: 15 mins

Finish your routine with planks, abdominal crunches, and a relaxing cool-down stretch.

Important Note: Always stay hydrated throughout your routine. Listen to your body and rest whenever you feel any sharp pain or unusual fatigue.
