💪 Why Your Body Needs Exercise

Your body is like a supercar — the more you use it, the stronger it gets!Kids who move for 60 minutes every dayare happier, sleep better, can do better at school

dancing, swimming, singing, football, yoga — anything that gets your heart beating faster counts. Keep moving and have fun!
